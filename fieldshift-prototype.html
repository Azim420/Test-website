The sample indicators suggest monitoring water availability and heat exposure. Recommendations below are general options to discuss with local agricultural advisers.

Suggested adaptation actions

1
Check soil moisture on the ground

Compare the satellite signal with field measurements at several points and at crop-root depth.

2
Review irrigation timing

If irrigation is available, review schedules with local guidance and prioritize crop growth stages that are most sensitive to water stress.

3
Monitor heat exposure

Track local weather forecasts and inspect crops for signs of heat stress during warmer periods.

4
Track vegetation changes

Compare vegetation indicators over time and investigate unusual changes with an on-site field check.

Important: decision support, not a diagnosis

Satellite products have different spatial resolutions, update frequencies, and uncertainty. FieldShift's prototype scores are not validated agricultural advice. Confirm satellite signals with local measurements, crop knowledge, and qualified agricultural guidance before making farm-management decisions.
